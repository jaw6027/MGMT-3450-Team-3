## ProfitLane product boundary

The student relays Angel's proposed direction: **AI-assisted dispatching in development**, with a **proposed 1–100 rating** considering **weight, distance, deadhead miles, diesel prices, pallet count and load payment**. This is owner-provided context, not verified implementation or a validated scoring system.

Market context could be a question for future product discussions or an educational companion to load review. The supplied information does **not** confirm that ProfitLane tracks live spot market rates, maintains lane histories, integrates external market data, forecasts rates, predicts market changes, automatically adjusts scores for geography or determines a profitable/fair rate. Do not add any of those as a working capability or promised future feature.

Questions for Angel can distinguish whether market information should remain something the dispatcher checks independently, be supplied manually as context, or be considered in a later concept. None of these alternatives is selected or approved by this research record. Keep the existing six proposed factors distinct from market topics, operating-expense topics and timing questions raised in the research.

**Message retained:** AI should be an assistant to dispatchers, not a replacement.
