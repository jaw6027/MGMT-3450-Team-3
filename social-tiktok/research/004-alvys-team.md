## ProfitLane opportunities and differentiation

These preserve the six opportunities supplied by the student while separating what is feasible now from later prerequisites:

1. **Short screen-recording demonstrations when features are ready.** Show one verified working task using fictional or approved sanitized data. If the workflow is still a design, maintain “Concept mockup · in development” throughout; do not present it as a working demo.
2. **Explain a feature's intended purpose.** Link the task to a question such as “Which inputs still need your review?” For a concept, use intended-benefit wording instead of claiming a measured improvement. Prefer a specific, observable workflow over “AI does everything.”
3. **Use Angel's experience honestly.** Ask him to approve his approximately eight years of trucking-industry experience and an authentic example. A short founder interview is feasible with a phone, steady framing and clear sound. Do not invent stories, customers or accomplishments.
4. **Develop original problem-and-solution explanations.** The earlier research suggests questions about headline payment, expenses, stops/time and market context. A fictional example can teach the review process now; do not claim the product solves every problem raised in those comments.
5. **Collect genuine testimonials later, with permission.** If a real customer or pilot participant shares feedback, accurately state their relationship, use approved wording and obtain permission before publishing. Disclose any material connection where applicable. Do not create placeholder customer quotes, use actors as undisclosed customers, or imply a future pilot already exists.
6. **Describe specific benefits with proportionate evidence.** If functional and confirmed, demonstrate exactly what an action does. If proposed, explain the intended support and limits. For any future time-saving claim, record a defined task, before/after method, comparable inputs, observation conditions and limitations; one person's outcome is not a general promise. No numerical benefit is currently established for ProfitLane.

**Candidate positioning:** “Review the inputs. Ask better questions. Keep the decision with the dispatcher.” This is suggested wording for later owner review, not a new approved tagline. Preserve the official tagline, **“Made for dispatchers by dispatchers.”**

ProfitLane can emphasize its intended dispatcher-assistance focus without claiming that competitors replace dispatchers, are less accurate, are harder to use or are more expensive. No evidence for those comparisons was supplied. Do not claim a unique or exclusive feature without verification.
