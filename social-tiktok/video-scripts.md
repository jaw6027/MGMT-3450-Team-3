## 5. Fuel math you can check

**Scheduled:** Day 10 · 2026-11-18

**Length:** 35–45 seconds

**Opening hook (2–3 seconds):** How much fuel does this fictional trip actually need?

**Full voiceover / speaking script:**

How much fuel does this fictional trip actually need? Start with 600 total miles. At an assumed six miles per gallon, that is 100 gallons. At an assumed four dollars per gallon, estimated fuel cost is $400. Change the MPG or the price and your estimate changes. This is a simplified example, not a fuel quote or a prediction for your truck. Review actual conditions and your own assumptions. Check the inputs before you use the result.

**Scenes and visuals:** 0–3s: calculator and total-mile card. 3–15s: 600 ÷ 6 = 100 gallons. 15–27s: 100 × $4 = $400. 27–40s: assumptions checklist.

**On-screen text:** Fictional example | 600 miles ÷ 6 MPG = 100 gal | 100 gal × $4 = $400 estimated fuel | Assumptions change results

**Caption:** A fuel estimate is only as good as its assumptions. Fictional inputs.

**Hashtags:** #ProfitLane #TruckDispatch #OwnerOperator #LoadPlanning
