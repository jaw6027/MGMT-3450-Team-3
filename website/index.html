01 / THE PROBLEM

The rate tells
half the story.

An offer can look good until you count the empty miles.

Deadhead, fuel, load weight, pallet count and the time involved all change the picture. Independent dispatchers have to weigh those details while keeping the conversation moving.

You know the driver, the route and what the numbers leave out. You deserve tools that help you put it together.

02 / THE PROPOSED SOLUTION

More context.
A fairer conversation.

Our proposed 1–100 fairness score is a way to bring the details of a load offer into view. The concept is in development; its formula and accuracy are not yet validated.

How this example is calculated

Total miles = loaded miles + deadhead. Estimated fuel cost = total miles ÷ 6.5 mpg × diesel price. Amount per mile after fuel = (payment − fuel cost) ÷ total miles.

The sample score is the rounded amount per mile after fuel ÷ $3 × 99 + 1, limited to 1–100. The $3 scale is arbitrary, not a market benchmark. Weight, pallets, wait time, tolls, region, equipment and other costs are excluded. This is not ProfitLane’s proposed production formula and does not establish a fair rate.
